At 0 HP roll a d20 at the start of each of your turns: 10+ = success. Three successes = stable; three failures = dead; natural 20 = back up with 1 HP. Any healing clears these.

Wild Shape

Action to transform · bonus action to revert · no casting, talking or item use · concentration keeps running · Stone’s Endurance still works. When beast HP hits 0 you revert and leftover damage hits Drogan — the tracker does this for you.
